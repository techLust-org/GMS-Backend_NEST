Request permanent removal of your personal data from GYMAXIS. This works whether or not you still have the app installed.

What happens when you delete your account

Your personal information is immediately and permanently removed:

  • Name, email, phone number, date of birth, gender
  • Profile photo and government ID document
  • Emergency contact and bio/specialization details
  • Saved login credentials — you will no longer be able to sign in

Records that other people depend on — such as your gym's attendance history, payment/ledger entries, and workout or diet plans tied to a trainer or gym — are retained in anonymized form for the gym's accounting and other members' continuity. If you own a gym, your gym is deactivated and its members/trainers will no longer be able to use it.
